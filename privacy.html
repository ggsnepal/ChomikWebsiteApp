Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

✦Information collection

Chomik fetches publicly available information from sites, mainly Roblox, to answer your commands. That information comes from the sites and is subject to their own policies. Chomik doesn’t cache information after it sends the output.

♥Data collection

Chomik saves:

  • Discord userIDs of whitelisted Chomik users.
  • Reports made using the /report command.

All messages and commands sent directly to Chomik or in servers are not logged. The only data processed from users are error logs, which are used to fix vulnerabilities and abuse. No other data is collected other than the ones stated above.
