What it does

Everything Chomik can do, grouped by what you’d actually use it for. Tap any command to see how it works.

–

Roblox lookups

Account ages, avatars, badges, friends, groups, game passes and more. Chomik reads public Roblox data and brings it back to chat.

    –

    Puzzles and OSINT

    Identify, decode and encode ciphers, solve anagrams, translate text, and trace where a string or an ID leads.

      –

      Image tricks

      Blur, pixelate, swirl, resize, read text from images and more, without leaving Discord.

        –

        AI helpers

        Ask questions, generate images, transcribe audio and turn text into speech.

          –

          Fun and games

          Leetspeak, coin flips, Wordle helpers and lookups for BlockTanks and Word Bomb.

            –

            Social and basics

            Look up TikTok, YouTube, Scratch and Pinterest, plus help, ping, invite and settings.

              Chomik is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord or Roblox.
