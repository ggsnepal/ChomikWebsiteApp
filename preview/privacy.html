Privacy policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Information collection

Chomik fetches publicly available information from sites, mainly Roblox, to answer your commands. That information comes from the sites and is subject to their own policies. Chomik does not cache information after it sends the output.

Data collection

Chomik saves:

  • Discord user IDs of whitelisted Chomik users.
  • Reports made with the /report command.

Messages and commands sent directly to Chomik or in servers are not logged. The only data processed from users is error logs, which are used to fix vulnerabilities and abuse. No other data is collected.
