FAQ

Good questions.

Is Chomik really free?

Yes. Every command is free to use.

What permissions does it need?

Only what's shown on the Discord invite screen. You can review and adjust them when you add the bot.

Where does the data come from?

From public sites and APIs, mainly Roblox. Chomik fetches and formats it; it doesn't access private accounts.

Something's not working. Where do I get help?

Check the status page first, then join the support server.
